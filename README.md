CGM Telemonitoring Review
Master's thesis — a PRISMA 2020 systematic review of continuous glucose monitoring (CGM)–enabled telemonitoring for adults with Type 2 diabetes, analysing both clinical effectiveness and health-system implementation across 35 studies (PICO + SPICE + CFIR + RE-AIM). Proposes the Triad Model (CGM + multidisciplinary care + AI decision support) and a macro/meso/micro policy roadmap for Taiwan's National Health Insurance.

Live page: https://nanghomnoonaye.github.io/CGM-Telemonitoring/
